Reef Sort is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game stores on your phone

Your progress (levels, stars, shells, pearls, outfits and settings) is saved on your device so you can keep playing.

Information collected by services the game uses

  • Ads (Google AdMob). When you choose to watch an ad for a reward, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address) and device information to show ads and measure them. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Purchases (Google Play). Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We only receive a confirmation that a purchase happened.
  • Phone backup (Android). If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may back up the game's progress to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. This is handled by Google.
  • App quality (Google Play, and Google Firebase if we add it). Information about crashes and how the game is used (for example, which level you reached) helps us fix bugs and improve the game. It is not used to identify you.

Your ad choices

In some places (such as the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states) the game asks for your choice about personalized ads the first time you open it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices inside the game. You can also turn off personalized ads for all apps in your phone's Google settings. Ads only ever play when you choose to watch one for a reward.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children

Reef Sort is not directed at children under 13. Ads in the game are set to be suitable for a general audience.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes the progress stored on your phone (a copy may remain in your Android phone backup until you delete it in your Google account). To ask us about or to delete any data linked to you, email reefsortgame@gmail.com and we'll handle it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
